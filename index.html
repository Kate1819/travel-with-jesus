    Chapter complete!

      Chapter 1: Come, follow me

      In Capernaum the player meets Jesus, asks Him questions, helps an old man carry water, watches Peter and Andrew leave their nets, and follows Jesus' footprints along the shore. Matthew 4:18–20, Mark 1:15, Matthew 25:40, Matthew 6:26.

      Chapter 2: Five loaves, two fish

      On the hillside the player trusts Jesus with a big problem, kindly asks a boy to share his lunch, serves bread to the crowd, remembers the family left out, and gathers twelve baskets. John 6:1–14, John 6:35.

      Chapter 3: The storm on the lake

      Out on the boat at night a storm hits. The player bails water with the disciples, learns they can't beat the storm alone, wakes Jesus, and sees Him calm the wind and waves. Mark 4:35–41, John 14:27.

      Chapter 4: Zacchaeus comes down

      In Jericho the player is kind to the man everyone dislikes, watches Jesus call him down from the tree, and helps Zacchaeus give back what he took. Luke 19:1–10, Luke 15:7.

      Chapter 5: He is risen!

      Early on Sunday the player walks with the women to the tomb, finds it empty, meets the risen Jesus, and runs to tell Peter and John. Matthew 28:1–10, 19–20, John 20:3–8.

      How choices work

      • No game over. A wrong choice brings gentle guidance from Jesus, then the child tries again.
      • Good choices earn kindness stars.
      • Jesus' words go into a verse journal. Quotes follow the NIV, shortened in a few places.
      • Every character speaks with a recorded natural voice, and each scene has its own music. Both can be switched off in the top corner.
